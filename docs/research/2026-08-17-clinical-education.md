# Clinical Education Literature: Cataract Surgical Skill Assessment

*Research agent report, 2026-08-17 (condensed; full citations in the report text below). Part of the feedback-metrics landscape survey.*

## Rubrics
- **OSACSS** (Saleh 2007): 20 items (14 task + 6 global), 5-point; anchors only at 1/3/5; construct validity; routinely used for masked VIDEO-based scoring → items ratable from microscope video.
- **ICO-OSCAR:phaco** (Golnik 2011): OSACSS steps + full behavioral anchors (Dreyfus). Complete item list extracted (incision, viscoelastic, rhexis start/formation, hydro, probe insertion, tip visibility/eye position, sculpt/chop, rotation, crack/segment removal, I/A cortex, IOL, wound; globals: wound neutrality, eye centration, tissue handling, spatial awareness, iris protection, speed/fluidity w/ 30/45/60-min anchors). **Video-observable: items 2, 4-13, 15-20.** Not: draping, OVD choice knowledge, foot-pedal/phacodynamics (unless overlay OCR), tactile IOP.
- **OASIS** (objective 1-pager: wound size, phaco time, total time, complications — "a manual version of what our system auto-extracts") + **GRASIS** (subjective global); mOSATS video applications.

## Quantitative correlates (key numbers)
- PhacoTrack vs masked OSACSS: **path length ρ=-0.68, movement count ρ=-0.67, total time ρ=-0.77**.
- PhacoTrainer 2025: probe path length r=-0.77, eye decentration r=-0.65 vs OSACSS items; attending/trainee separation on 8 categories.
- CatSkill 2025: 3 video-only metrics (limbus-Purkinje neutrality, centration, focus) per phase → RF **AUC 0.865** attending vs resident.
- Rhexis: ideal ~4.5-5.5mm, centered, 360° overlap; errant rhexis → vitreous loss; overlap → PCO; ≥0.3mm decentration degrades aspheric IOLs; WetCat computable formulas (4πA/P², limbus-normed centration, Fourier smoothness); Capsulotomy Proficiency Index separates skill; machine scores match experts (PubMed 38560333).
- CDE falls with experience (55.4→30.1 within residency; >160 cases to expert level) — **device telemetry, not video** (unless overlay OCR).
- Duration: residents +5-19 min; independent predictor of early post-op outcomes.
- Complications: resident PCR 3.4% vs faculty 1.4%; decline after ~70 cases; UK NOD case-mix-adjusted PCR model; Birkmeyer NEJM 2013 anchors skill-ratings→outcomes.

## Programs & the gap
ACGME: 86-case minimum (volume, not quality); Milestones 2.0 = coarse semiannual committee levels; EPAs in only ~22% of programs (time = top barrier); documented faculty-resident feedback perception mismatch; expert video review 8.5 days vs crowd 9 hours for 28 videos.

## What feedback works
Video coaching RCT: +3.36 OSATS (p=0.007); EyeSi proficiency gating: errant rhexis 15.7%→5.0% (McCannel 2013) and **task-specific transfer** (only rhexis-related vitreous loss fell → per-step feedback is the mechanism); UK NOD: 38% PCR reduction with EyeSi access; wet-lab+video curricula: junior PC tears 3.07%→1.13%; ICO pattern: score at case end, discuss immediately, trainee self-scores video.

## Five highest-value video-computable metrics (education view)
1. **Rhexis geometry** (diameter, circularity, centration, rhexis-optic overlap) — ICO item 5, best outcome links, computable formulas exist.
2. **Instrument path length / movement count / smoothness per phase** — strongest replicated expert-rating correlations (ρ≈-0.7).
3. **Eye centration/stability** (limbus vs frame center) — items 15-16, CatSkill AUC 0.865, cheap given our limbus masks.
4. **Total + per-phase durations + idle** — ρ=-0.77, explicit rubric anchors, outcome predictor, our norms substrate.
5. **Second-instrument presence/positioning + bimanual coordination** — items 7/8/11/18, doubles as safety-event detection.

Design implication: metrics land when delivered **per step, immediately, against anchors and cohort norms, inside video review** (McCannel task-specificity + ICO debrief workflow) — i.e., phase-indexed metric-annotated video review is the right product shape.
